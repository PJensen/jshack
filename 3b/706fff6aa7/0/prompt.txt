Lets plan out the new and improved targeting model!



- Persistent hostile target selection: tap a distant hostile or use Tab/Shift+Tab.
- Ranged activation fires immediately at a valid locked target.
- Without a lock, ranged activation opens temporary point targeting.
- Point targeting selects coordinates, not creatures.
- The rules ray resolves the first actor, wall, or ground impact.
- Ammo behavior runs from that resolved impact, enabling true area denial.
- Wands share the same target-lock versus point-target distinction.
- No ranged keyboard-direction or Enter-confirm system; keyboard uses WoW-style target selection.

# Unified Tactical Ranged Targeting

## Summary

Replace ranged auto-nearest behavior with two explicit paths:

- A persistent hostile target lock selected by tapping an enemy or Tab/Shift+Tab.
- A temporary point-targeting mode opened when ranged activation has no lock.

Locked targets fire immediately. Point targeting selects coordinates by pointer tap; keyboard does not use ranged direction keys or Enter confirmation.

## Implementation Changes

- Add an ECS target-lock component for the controlled actor, storing the selected hostile entity.
  - Target locks persist through range/LOS failure.
  - Dead, removed, or no-longer-hostile targets are cleared.
  - Firing at an unusable lock reports the failure and does not silently retarget.

- Extend the targeting controller:
  - Tap a hostile creature to select it without firing.
  - Tab/Shift+Tab cycles visible hostile targets and updates the persistent lock.
  - Preserve ordinary tap-to-move for non-hostile tiles.
  - Keep point targeting transient and pointer-committed.
  - Escape cancels temporary point targeting; no ranged keyboard cursor movement or Enter confirmation.

- Update ranged activation:
  - Validate the equipped bow or wand.
  - If a valid lock exists, dispatch an immediate ranged action against that entity.
  - Otherwise open point targeting using the weapon/spell range.
  - Remove nearest-enemy auto-selection.
  - Preserve AI and explicit programmatic target dispatch compatibility.

- Refactor ranged projectile resolution around a physical ray:
  - The intent carries an endpoint coordinate and may optionally carry a locked target.
  - Traverse the ray in order.
  - The first actor, blocking wall, or terminal ground tile becomes the resolved impact.
  - Actors—including friendly and neutral NPCs—are physical projectile collisions and consume the shot without a ranged confirmation dialog.
  - A locked target establishes the aim endpoint but never bypasses intervening collisions.

- Split projectile content hooks into distinct impact semantics:
  - `onProjectileActorImpact`
  - `onProjectileWallImpact`
  - `onProjectileGroundImpact`
  - Keep miss resolution for combat misses, but route the final miss location through the same terminal-impact model.
  - Pass the resolved impact coordinate to every relevant ammo hook.
  - This gives chalk, fire, and future bomb arrows a real ground-impact surface for area denial.

- Make wand routing use authored targeting mode:
  - Creature-targeted wand/spell actions use the persistent hostile lock.
  - Point/area-targeted wand/spell actions open coordinate targeting without a lock.
  - Self-targeted actions remain immediate.
  - Do not infer targeting from nearest-enemy proximity.

- Add bridge/display projection for the locked target so the UI can show the current hostile target and the point-targeting reticle can show the actual terminal impact tile when a wall or actor blocks the ray.

## Test Plan

- Target-lock selection:
  - Tap hostile selects without firing.
  - Tab/Shift+Tab cycles deterministically.
  - Lock persists when temporarily out of range or LOS.
  - Invalid/dead/faction-changed locks clear.

- Activation behavior:
  - Valid lock fires immediately.
  - No lock opens point targeting.
  - Pointer tile commit dispatches coordinates.
  - Keyboard direction keys and Enter never fire ranged attacks.
  - Non-hostile taps retain movement behavior.

- Projectile resolution:
  - Empty tile produces ground impact.
  - Wall terminates the ray.
  - Friendly/neutral/hostile actors terminate the ray.
  - Intervening actors block a locked target.
  - Combat misses resolve to actor, wall, or ground through the same impact model.

- Ammo and wand behavior:
  - Chalk applies to actor, wall, and ground impacts.
  - Fire arrows can attach future ground/area-denial effects without ranged-system changes.
  - Wand targeting follows authored creature, point/area, or self mode.
  - Existing AI ranged attacks and explicit `rules.rangedAttack` callers remain valid.

- Run focused ranged, chalk, wand/use, targeting, interaction, event, architecture, and determinism tests.

## Assumptions

- Touch/pointer is the commit mechanism for point-targeted ranged actions.
- A persistent lock is gameplay state modeled as an ECS component, not controller-local state or `world[...]`.
- Locked-target firing does not automatically fall back to another enemy.
- Core ray and targeting infrastructure comes first; fire-arrow area denial and bomb-arrow content are follow-up authored behaviors.
