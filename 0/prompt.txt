General Class Updates

** of the following ideas, which is something that we can do to have an outsize impact, specifically, I'm looking to enhance the game by making more dimensionality, or at the very least to surface mechanics that are oftentimes buried under the sheer breadth of the game.

The ideas are as follows:

Want to make a shaman class?

# Shaman

- dungeon mushrooms have a very different effect on them, unclear precisely how we can do this type of behavioral override without making a mess.
- they commune with the forces of nature (lightning, fire, wind, water)
- they carry a talisman

What about the druid class?

# Druid

> we're going to modify the druid along the following lines

- they commune with mother nature, gaia
- druids start with a spell that allows them to tame creatures (the current scroll stays where it is, but the underlying "spell" if you will becomes a formal spell that is granted to the druid)

Any other classes while we're thinking about it?

# Knight

- devoted, light, sunsword, shield, heavy plate armor
- limited healing (note: we need some kind of healing suppression stat) -- damage is well centralized, but I don't know that healing follows a similar trajectory.

## General

- it's rare to be encumbered, I don't think I've ever had a run where I was encumbered, we need to tweak encumberence to make it more realistic, right now it feels like players can carry the world
