## Summary
Give potion-derived lethargy and paralysis distinct status-effect VIS badges, without changing their gameplay mechanics or potion inventory glyphs.

## Current behavior
- `potion_lethargy` (display name: **Potion of Sluggishness**) applies `lethargic` for 30 turns and reduces stamina regeneration by 0.5/turn when drunk. Its splash and weapon coating apply `slowed`, which already has a 🐌 VIS badge.
- `potion_paralysis` applies `stun`: 10 turns when drunk, 5 on splash, and 4 on a successful weapon-coating proc (40% chance). Its status currently displays as the generic 💫 Stunned badge.
- VIS mappings are in `src/display/ui/hud.js`; the shared status projection is in `src/main/ui/effectRows.js` and `src/main/ui/statusDisplayMetadata.js`.

## Desired VIS entries
- **Lethargic:** 🦥 (`U+1F9A5`), key `lethargic`.
- **Paralyzed:** 🔒 (`U+1F512`), visually distinct from ordinary `stunned`.
- Preserve existing **Slowed** 🐌 and ordinary **Stunned** 💫 entries.

## Implementation requirements
1. Add the 🦥 Lethargic VIS entry so drinking `potion_lethargy` displays it for the effect's lifetime.
2. Display 🔒 Paralyzed for stun effects caused by `potion_paralysis` (oral, splash, and weapon coating), but continue to display unrelated stuns as 💫 Stunned.
3. Retain or introduce enough effect-source provenance for the read-only VIS projection to distinguish paralysis-origin stun. The coating path currently records `stun` without an explicit source tag; address that without altering the actual stun rules.
4. Keep the item/palette glyphs and existing combat, stacking, duration, persistence, and removal behavior unchanged.
5. Add regression coverage for both badges, all paralysis delivery paths, generic stun remaining 💫, and lethargy splash/coating remaining 🐌.

## Acceptance criteria
- Drinking Potion of Sluggishness shows 🦥 Lethargic, not a generic fallback.
- Drinking, splashing, or successfully striking with a paralysis-coated weapon produces 🔒 Paralyzed while the stun is active.
- Non-paralysis stun remains 💫 Stunned; `slowed` remains 🐌 Slowed.
- Badge timers track the underlying effects, and no duplicated/phantom statuses appear.
