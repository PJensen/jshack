# Selected Potion Handler Pass

## Summary

Implement the four selected potion upgrades only: `potion_vigor`, `potion_adrenaline`, `potion_keen_edge`, and `potion_lethargy`. Use existing item hooks, weapon coatings, `ItemInfo.bonuses`, status effects, and hazard spawning. No new UI, verb, or broad alchemy system.

## Key Changes

- Add new weapon coating definitions in `weaponCoatings.js`:
  - `lifedraw`: consumes 1 charge on hit, heals attacker for 1 HP on proc.
  - `adrenaline`: consumes 1 charge on hit, restores a small stamina amount to attacker.
  - `lethargy`: consumes 1 charge on hit, applies `slowed` to defender.
- Add dip hooks in potion content:
  - `potion_vigor`: weapon-only dip, grants `lifedraw` coating, about 8 charges.
  - `potion_adrenaline`: weapon-only dip, grants `adrenaline` coating, about 8 charges.
  - `potion_lethargy`: weapon/ammo dip, grants `lethargy` coating, about 6 charges.
  - `potion_keen_edge`: weapon-only dip, permanently increments `ItemInfo.bonuses.critChance` by `0.02`, stackable, consumes potion, emits `item:applied`.
- Upgrade throw behavior:
  - `potion_vigor`: spawn a `blood_pool` floor hazard for a short duration; on hazard tick, living creatures heal lightly and undead take holy/life damage.
  - `potion_adrenaline`: thrown splash applies `berserk` to living enemies on the landing tile/radius.
  - `potion_lethargy`: keep/confirm sticky slow splash behavior using existing `slowed` status; if feasible, spawn a short `sticky_syrup` floor hazard that applies slow each tick.
