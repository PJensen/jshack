## Bug

A deity was **wrathful** and still granted the player a **boon**. Divine favor should not ignore the deity's current disposition.

## Code-grounded thesis

- `src/rules/systems/praySystem.js` has a fast prayer-boon resolver with base chance, distress/pity/drought bonuses, and even a death's-door bonus. **It never checks the deity's mood or standing** before calling `applyPrayerBoon()`.
- That same prayer path can **uncurse equipped gear before the boon roll**, likewise without a standing check.
- `src/rules/systems/deitySystem.js` separately handles `miracle` callbacks. Verify whether that route can also deliver benefits during wrath. Don't fix one producer while leaving another open.
- Existing code already reads `deity._queryPrecise()` for standing decisions, and `curse:equipment` in `curseHooks.js` provides a canonical curse path.
