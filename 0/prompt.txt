## Summary

The simulation emits rich `healed` and `hazard:pulse` events, but the visual projection layer does not provide clear, distinct feedback for them.

Examples include `blood_pool` pulses healing living creatures or damaging undead, as well as healing from potions and regeneration. Without an event-specific visual treatment, players may mistake healing for combat damage or cosmetic splatter.

## Suggested direction

Add clear visual cues for healing and hazard pulses, such as floating numbers, distinct tint or flash effects, and/or pulse rings. Ensure healing is visually distinguishable from combat damage and cosmetic effects, while communicating the outcome of hazard pulses for different creature types.
