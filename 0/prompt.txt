I want to make a new, specialized type of trap, this one is called the arrow trap. it is a combination pressure plate, and an aimed arrow shot directly at the pressure plate. what makes this trap unique is that it resets every turn.

also interesting is that since this trap resets, it can be used in an adversarial way by luring monsters toward the trap. 

in an ideal situation, the arrows themselves are kind of nasty -- instead of flat damage, we could use all sorts of nasty things.

similar variant might magic missle trap but that can be left for a later exploration. we can start simply, simple arrows even.

the challenge here is that this is a two part trap, the plate, and an arrow that comes from adjacent wall -- I dont know that we need a full on shooter, perhaps that the arrow shoots from either left, right, top, down -- nearest wall.

---

I do not like `resetsEveryTurn: true` why not `resetsEvery: 1` to make for something generically applicable.
